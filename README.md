# Week 4: Comprehensive Data Science Report and Insights Presentation Plan

## TelePulse AI — Executive Capstone Report, Business Insights & Non-Technical Communication Strategy

### Project Overview
This capstone deliverable consolidates the entire TelePulse AI initiative into a polished, executive-ready report. It translates complex predictive churn modeling into high-impact business decisions, featuring mock-up visualizations, actionable customer segmentation, and a structured presentation plan for non-technical stakeholders.

### Deliverable Contents
- **Executive Summary** — $1.84M net ARR savings, churn reduction 26.5% → 14.8%, 340% first-year ROI
- **Methodology Review** — Independent synthesis of data ingestion, quality governance, modeling strategy, and threshold tuning
- **Core Business Insights** — Month-to-month contract fragility (42.7% churn), Fiber support paradox, 90-day onboarding cliff, electronic check friction
- **Actionable Segmentation Matrix** — 2×2 Risk vs Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) matrix with targeted intervention playbooks
- **Non-Technical Storytelling** — Minto Pyramid Principle, "Answer First" executive narrative, technical-to-business vocabulary translation table
- **Strategic Recommendations** — 3-phase 12-month post-deployment roadmap (Pilot → Automation → Next-Gen AI)
